💡 Why Time Compression?

This allows AI agents to work at accelerated speeds while maintaining realistic workflow patterns. A product that would take humans weeks can be completed in hours, but with proper planning, review cycles, and quality gates.

Example: Build a full CRUD app with authentication:

  • Human team: 2-3 weeks
  • Agent team: 8-12 real hours (8-12 agent days)
